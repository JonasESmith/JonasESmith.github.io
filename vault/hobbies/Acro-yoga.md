My partner and I started practicing [acro-yoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acroyoga) at a climbing gym, and we fell in love with the skill progression and all the fun we were able to have.

We've met some incredible friends along the way and helped start an amazing community in Arkansas. We teach acro-yoga at [Climb Bentonville](https://theclimbgyms.com/bentonville/) every Monday!
