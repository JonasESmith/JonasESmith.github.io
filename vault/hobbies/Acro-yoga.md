My partner and I started practicing [acro-yoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acroyoga) at a climbing gym, and we fell in love with the skill progression and all the fun we were able to have. It is both a workout and an incredible hobby. I rarely laugh as hard as I do than when I am working on different Acro Skills with a partner.

We've met some incredible friends along the way and helped start an amazing community in Arkansas where wheree teach acro-yoga at [Climb Bentonville](https://theclimbgyms.com/bentonville/) every Monday! We have a range of skill levels and bodies that come in the doors. And I love being able to help people learn how strong they actually are.
