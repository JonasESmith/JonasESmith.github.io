Surprisingly enough I think drawing `( or any creative outlet )` really helps in day to day life. Weather it is designing out an application, drawing up floor plans for my van build, or deciding how to re-arange my room. Having the ability to transfer over styles, shades, and general constructs from my brain to some holding medium is incredibly helpful.
