I've been drawing for as long as I can remember, but as a teenager and adult I really fell in love with capturing portraits.

![[portrait_study_2016.png|portrait study, ~2016]]

Surprisingly enough, I think drawing (or any creative outlet) really helps in day-to-day life. Whether it's designing an application, drawing up floor plans for my van build, or deciding how to rearrange my room, being able to move styles, shading, and general constructs from my head onto some medium is incredibly helpful.
