 I started practicing [Acryoyoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acroyoga) with my partner at a climbing gym, and we fell in love with the skill progression and all the fun we were able to have.
We have met some incredible friends along the way, and have been able to start an amazing community in Arkansas. Where we teach acro-yoga at [Climb Bentonville](https://theclimbgyms.com/bentonville/) every Monday!
